At that pinned position (the top of Stage 3's bullet set) the remove-or-keep duty is above the insertion point, but the cost-pricing duty ("Price the witnessed cost from records before pricing the urgency", `agents/skills/investigate/SKILL.md` Stage 3, fourth top-level bullet) sits below it. ## Expected

The landed lens bullet's duty references resolve from the bullet's own position: either the positional adverb is dropped ("through the remove-or-keep and cost-pricing duties"), or the bullet is positioned after the cost-pricing bullet so "above" is true for both named duties.

## Suggested direction

Drop the word "above" from the prescribed bullet text in the plan's Task 1 (one-word edit; the Terms entry "cost-pricing duty" already names the duty without position), then re-verify the em-dash gate and the pins-suite failing-set invariance per the plan's standing constraints.
