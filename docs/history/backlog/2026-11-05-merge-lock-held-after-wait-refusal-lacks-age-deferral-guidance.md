## Observed versus expected

Observed: the authoring self-landing (`land_squash.sh land-worktree`) hit the swap-critical-section merge lock held by a live foreign session and spent two full bounded waits (300 seconds each) before deferring. The refusal prose for the held-after-wait exit (`scripts/land_squash.sh`, the rc-1 branch: "retry the landing once the holder releases (the retry re-runs the full pre-lock gate sequence)") is unconditional - it sends the blocked session into an identical blind wait regardless of how old the hold already is. The helper's own exhaustion evidence already carries the fact that would distinguish the cases (`done-lock.sh` prints `lock held: token=... label=... repo=... age=...s` on exhaustion, echoed by the wrapper as `swap-lock:` lines), but nothing consumes it: a hold age already far exceeding the swap critical section's design span (parentage verification, compare-and-swap, post-landing gate, reconcile tail - seconds, per the span-narrowing commit 1a1673921) means the holder is not in a short swap section and no bounded wait sized for one can ever clear it. The session retried once anyway (per the guidance), waited another blind 300 seconds, and then deferred with the `pending_landing` state record - the deferral machinery itself worked end to end (record, retain branch/worktree/claim, later resume, rebase on the moved tip, digest-verified landing dd1803d2c), but roughly ten minutes of blind waiting and one deferred turn were spent discovering a fact the evidence already stated at the first exhaustion.

Expected: the held-after-wait refusal reads the hold age from the acquire evidence it already captured and branches its guidance on it. When the age already exceeds the wait window just spent (the holder predates the wait and stayed live through it - a long-span holder, for example a foreign caller-held flow), the refusal says so and directs the deferral path (the `pending_landing` record, retain, retry on a later turn) instead of the generic retry. When the age is within or near the wait window (a genuine short swap section that may clear shortly), the existing one-retry guidance stands. Guidance and evidence only: no lock semantics change, no new state, no gate.
