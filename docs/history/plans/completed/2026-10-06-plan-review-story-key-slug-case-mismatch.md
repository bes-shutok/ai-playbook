## Terms

- Story key: the uppercase ticket prefix of a plan filename (for example `STORY-KEY`), preserved in plan filenames, titles, and links as the user-facing identifier.
- Feature slug: the plan filename stem minus its leading `YYYY-MM-DD-` prefix, case-preserving, as derived today by `feature_slug()` in scripts/plan_readiness.py (line 218) and mirrored in scripts/docs_branch_plan_guard.py (lines 67 through 70).
- Canonical artifact slug (new): the feature slug lowercased; the one machine identity for plan-review artifacts. Derived by the new `artifact_slug()` in scripts/plan_readiness.py, which reports a named derivation refusal when the lowercased form is not already a safe kebab token under corrections_record's `SEAL_SLUG_RE` shape, never sanitizing by other transforms; the refusal surfaces as a named readiness failure, never an uncaught traceback.
- Case-variant record: a round record or record half under the reviews home whose filename matches the canonical discovery shape modulo letter case (case-insensitive fullmatch) but not exactly; the only legacy state that exists in practice, since pre-change story-key pairs were named with the uppercase feature slug. A record half (an orphan `.md` without its `.stats.json` or the reverse) matches at the filename level like any record, and the rename remedy names both halves of a pair.
- Exec-review record series: the execution-run review series `*-plan-review-<plan-slug>-exec-r<N>.md` keyed by the case-preserving plan slug in scripts/done_sweep_gates_lib.py (`_plan_slug`, lines 3918 through 3923) and matched by the same case-preserving grammar in scripts/reverse_squash_guard.py (`EXEC_REVIEW_RECORD_RE`, lines 132 through 135); out of this plan's scope and unchanged in both consumers, so an uppercase-story plan's exec records keep today's naming.
- Discovery shape: the shared glob and regex skeletons `*-plan-review-{slug}-r*.md` and `.*-plan-review-{slug}-r([1-9]\d*)\.md` owned by `review_round_shape()` in scripts/plan_readiness.py (comment block and skeletons at lines 224 through 233), instantiated with the canonical artifact slug from this plan onward.
- Seal path: `docs/history/review-certs/<artifact-slug>.md`, derived by `seal_relpath()` in scripts/corrections_record.py (line 365) under the lowercase kebab shape gate `SEAL_SLUG_RE` (line 95).

## Why

The readiness path derives a case-preserving slug while the corrections and seal path demands a lowercase kebab slug, so a plan whose filename carries an uppercase story key makes the same identity illegal at one gate and mandatory at the other. Witnessed 2026-10-06: readiness derived `STORY-KEY-nightly-bitmap-rebuild-generation-lifecycle` from the plan filename and rejected the lowercase sidecar slug; `validate_review_staging.py --hard` at the corrections and seal path refuses any slug outside the kebab shape; a consumer's case-only rename of the plan to lowercase then passed readiness but the closeout gate discovered the old uppercase path from the staged rename and failed corrections verification for it. The version-1 generic sidecar validation checks `artifact_slug` only as a scalar string (scripts/validate_review_staging.py lines 4638 through 4642), so a pair can be valid under `--hard` and still fail readiness name binding: the two gates genuinely disagree, exactly as the origin row records.

The slug flows through surfaces that must resolve one identity: round filename discovery and per-round name binding (scripts/plan_readiness.py `latest_review_round`, `check_review_name`, and the plans skill's post-round probe), the readiness evidence payload's seal-context slug (scripts/plan_readiness.py line 1366, consumed by the staging validator's graded stale arm whose slug precedence sits at lines 4190 through 4200), the seal path itself (scripts/corrections_record.py), the execution runtime's three scope-recovery discovery sites (scripts/execute_plan_runtime.py lines 11271, 11618, 11894; the recover-task-scope site's composition described by the runtime contract's list at agents/skills/execute-plan/runtime-contract.md line 403, the requeued and plan-edit sites described at lines 405 and 407 without naming the derivation), and the docs-branch certified-plan guard's mirrored derivation (scripts/docs_branch_plan_guard.py lines 33 through 39 and 67 through 70). Guidance spans dictate the round naming to authors (agents/skills/plans/SKILL.md lines 194, 601, and 620, agents/skills/review-plan/SKILL.md lines 132, 286, and 383, agents/skills/review-staging/SKILL.md lines 261 and 272) and would keep teaching the divergent rule, including one span that states the opposite rule outright ("Slugs preserve their case.", agents/skills/review-staging/SKILL.md line 272) beside a record-selection helper whose slug pattern admits uppercase (scripts/review_record_selection.py line 110).

Inventory (witnessed 2026-10-06 against the primary reviews home and the seal directory): every existing round-pair slug and every seal filename is already lowercase; the only uppercase matches in the reviews home are backup-timestamp suffixes, not slugs. No legacy record needs migration here, and the lowercase rule is a no-op for every current all-lowercase plan filename. The uppercase witness lives in the consumer repository, not in this corpus.

## Outcome

- One canonical derivation: `artifact_slug(plan_path)` = `feature_slug(plan_path)` lowercased; every machine identity surface (discovery glob, name binding, sidecar equality, evidence payload slug, seal path including the graded arm's plan-family fallback, runtime scope recovery, docs-branch guard matching) resolves through it, while feature-slug case stays preserved in refusal prose and user-facing identifiers.
- A story-key plan (uppercase feature slug) binds a lowercase round pair and its lowercase sidecar `artifact_slug`, passes readiness, and resolves its certification seal at the lowercase name: the witnessed STORY-KEY-shaped failure cannot recur.
- Case-variant records refuse rather than guess, through one case-insensitive secondary probe over the reviews home: when canonical-shaped AND case-variant rounds both exist, readiness returns the named case-variant collision refusal; when canonical discovery is empty and only case-variant rounds exist, readiness returns a named refusal directing the one-time rename of the legacy pair to the canonical shape (a legacy pair cannot pass the gates anyway: its pre-change sidecar declares the uppercase feature slug, which the seal shape gate refuses); all other machine surfaces (the per-round probe, the runtime discovery sites) stay canonical-only and fail closed, with readiness owning the case-variant diagnosis and remedy.
- The closeout case-only-rename arm narrows to identity: the docs-branch guard's exact-filename shared-name intersection is retained as-is, and its certification witness resolves for story-key-named plans where today it silently skips (the certified-`None` skip arm means an uppercase-named plan with a lowercase-declaring sidecar misses the exact sidecar-slug match and is skipped, not compared); post-change the witness resolves and the digest comparison actually runs (fixture-pinned, both roots carrying the same uppercase story filename). A transitional cross-root rename (uppercase in the branch root, lowercase incoming) keeps the intersection empty and stays silently skipped; that state is owned by the corrections anchor leg's exact artifact-path comparison, not this plan. The residual rename cost is recorded, not dissolved: a certification seal's anchor entry stores the artifact relpath at certification time, so a post-certification case-only rename still refuses at the corrections anchor leg (scripts/corrections_record.py compares artifact paths exactly); the remedy stays reverting the rename or re-certifying, and the refusal is named, never silent.
- The validator contract distinction stays explicit: version-1 generic validation keeps checking `artifact_slug` as a scalar string only; the lowercase kebab shape stays enforced at the corrections and seal path. No new generic schema error is added and no existing refusal class is removed.
- Two plans whose feature slugs differ only in story-key case normalize to the same canonical slug and share one review identity; sidecars carry no plan identity, so detecting that aliasing would need a schema change and stays an accepted, documented residual (stated in the guidance span, not encoded).
- Gate delta: two net-new named refusal shapes in readiness (the case-variant family: collision and rename direction), plus the unsafe-stem derivation refusal, which is today's seal-gate refusal re-surfaced earlier through the named fail path rather than a new refusal class; one new derivation helper, one validator graded-arm fallback rewrite (delegation switch, helper rename, catch conversion, docstring amendment), and mirrored consumer switches; no outcome-contract change, no schema change, no seal-path rule change, and no sibling-handshake version bump (the new helper is derived state on the readiness side only, the validator's stale-sibling attribute failure keeps degrading through the existing narrow except posture, and the handshake's named-failure contract is untouched).

## Gist & Examples

TLDR: the plan filename keeps its uppercase story key for humans, while every review artifact resolves one lowercase canonical slug derived from it; discovery, binding, seals, runtime recovery, and the docs-branch guard all switch to that one derivation, case-variant legacy states are refused by name with the rename remedy, and the witnessed rename-then-closeout failure narrows to a named, remedy-bearing anchor-leg refusal.

Before (today): a plan `2026-10-06-STORY-KEY-nightly-bitmap-rebuild.md` derives feature slug `STORY-KEY-nightly-bitmap-rebuild`; readiness demands the sidecar say exactly that, the seal path refuses it as unsafe, the author is stuck between two illegal choices, and after renaming the plan to lowercase the closeout still trips over the staged uppercase path.

After (this plan): the same plan derives canonical artifact slug `STORY-KEY-nightly-bitmap-rebuild`; the round pair is written `2026-10-06-plan-review-STORY-KEY-nightly-bitmap-rebuild-r1.md` plus sidecar with `artifact_slug: STORY-KEY-nightly-bitmap-rebuild`; `check_review_name` binds it; readiness passes; the corrections arm resolves the seal at `docs/history/review-certs/STORY-KEY-nightly-bitmap-rebuild.md`. An all-lowercase plan (this corpus's convention) derives the same slug as today, so nothing existing changes. A story-key plan with leftover case-variant round pairs gets a named refusal: both shapes present means the case-variant collision reason; canonical-empty means the rename direction naming the legacy pair. A case-only plan rename after certification still refuses at the corrections anchor leg by exact artifact path, named as today, with revert-or-recertify as the remedy.

Edge cases pinned: a plan stem that lowercases to an unsafe kebab form returns the named derivation refusal through the readiness fail path (`OUTCOME: fail` with the filename named), never a traceback; an all-lowercase plan's artifact slug equals its feature slug byte for byte; the CLI name-check print names both the story slug and the canonical slug; a guard digest comparison across a case-only plan rename accepts on identical canonical identity; a genuinely uppercase declared sidecar slug keeps failing the seal shape gate exactly as today; the graded arm's context-less plan-family fallback resolves the lowercase seal; a renamed-to-lowercase plan with legacy uppercase-shaped pairs alone gets the rename direction, not a bare "no review artifact".

## Evaluation Criteria

**Quality dimensions:**
- correctness: every machine surface listed in the Why section resolves the canonical derivation; no surface re-derives the rule privately except the guard's declared mirror; the case-variant probe's three outcomes (both shapes, canonical-empty, clean) are exhaustive over the states the probe can observe.
- fail-closed shape: an unsafe plan stem, a both-shapes state, and a canonical-empty case-variant state are named refusals with `OUTCOME: fail`, never silent normalizations, never tracebacks; no lenient binding arm exists for case-variant pairs.
- no weakening: the seal shape gate, the version-1 generic string-only check, the digest rules, and every existing refusal class keep their contracts byte for byte; the plan adds no schema field and removes no check.
- testability: each new behavior pins through a fixture with both the pass and the fail shape in the touched suite's own style; the witnessed STORY-KEY shape is a first-class fixture (uppercase story filename to lowercase round pair to seal resolution).

**Anti-goals:**
- No bulk rename or migration tooling over existing records.
- No plan-filename renaming, no story-key case-folding in user-facing prose or titles.
- No new generic schema error on `artifact_slug`, no new telemetry, no new gate beyond the named case-variant refusal family.
