## Gist TLDR

TLDR: add two prose fidelity duties to the review orchestrator's contract so record pollution is caught at the step that creates it: Step 3 verifies a finding's quoted evidence literal actually occurs in the reviewed artifact before staging it, and Step 5 re-derives the plan's accounting surfaces after every fold batch so propagation misses surface at zero extra rounds instead of a round later.

Both origins are witnessed: a 2026-09-30 panel round staged a High blocking finding whose entire evidence was a quoted command string that occurs nowhere in the reviewed artifact (mis-transcribed from memory; disk truth discarded it only after staging), and the 2026-09-30 review loop for `docs/history/plans/completed/2026-09-30-maintenance-loop-interruption-resume-and-cycle-harvest.md` (origin 2's witnessing loop) paid two extra rounds (its r2 and r4) to fold-batch propagation misses that the fold batch itself could have caught. Both fixes are sentences in the orchestrator's existing numbered lists; the reconciliation skill's fold batch gains the same probe for its own trigger class. No script, schema, refusal class, or gate changes.

## Outcome + Gate delta

Review rounds stop staging findings whose quoted evidence does not exist in the reviewed artifact: the synthesis step discards them (`excerpt-mismatch`) or returns them for re-anchoring before the sidecar is written, so the staged record never carries a fabricated-evidence row. Fold batches stop leaking accounting drift into the next round: the Review Scope lists, the Gate delta count, the read-only list, the pinned Validation literals, and the Evaluation Criteria's checkbox ownership are re-derived mechanically as part of every fold, and a reconciliation triggered by that failure class re-runs the same probe before declaring `reconciled`.

Gate delta: two numbered duties added to `agents/skills/review-plan/SKILL.md` (Step 3 item 5 quoted-literal verification; Step 5 item 6 accounting-surface re-derivation, each renumbering its trailing items), one Fold-propagation probe paragraph in `agents/skills/review-reconciliation/SKILL.md`'s Output contract, one caller-handoff trigger-vocabulary extension (fold-propagation joins the handoff template's trigger enumeration), and two bidirectional Integration Points attestation sentences (one per skill); no script, schema, refusal class, hard gate, fence, or protocol layer is added.

## Terms

- **Fold batch**: the set of plan edits made in one Step 5 pass to fold one round's accepted findings (review-plan Step 5 items 1 through 5).
- **Accounting surfaces**: the plan-level bookkeeping regions that must stay in sync with task checkboxes: the Review Scope editable-region list, the Review Scope read-only list, the Outcome `Gate delta:` count, the Validation Commands' pinned literals, and the Evaluation Criteria (every criterion remains owned by at least one task checkbox).
- **Quoted literal**: a string a finding's evidence quotes as occurring in the reviewed artifact (a command, a prose anchor sentence, a code span).

- No script, schema, or vocabulary change ships: the only touched files are the two skill files, and the staging vocabulary's existing `excerpt-mismatch` code is reused, not extended.
