---
name: self-heal
description: >
  Recover useful progress when a workflow is about to stop on a blocker, repeated review cycles,
  a failed tool, or another obstacle that may have a remedy. Diagnose first, find the smallest
  safe workaround, and carry it out within the caller's authority and existing gates. Use before
  judging the stop unrecoverable, pausing, or asking the user for direction; it cannot replace a
  required user decision or authorization.

# Self-heal

Continue required work directly when its next action is authorized and available. Invoke self-heal before stopping when an evidenced obstacle prevents that action, even without an explicit tool refusal. Preserve the caller's scope, permissions, hard gates, worker-liveness rules, retry budget, and completion boundary.

## Recovery

1. State the concrete blocker, its evidence, and the exact next action it prevents. Read the relevant workflow instructions and inspect the source of the refusal; do not infer that it is unavoidable from the refusal message alone. Compare the caller's current lifecycle stage and next action with each requirement's source-defined activation point, and name that source when explaining the classification. Keep a requirement blocking when the next action depends on it or an existing user decision, external-owner action, safety, authorization, or other gate applies now. When its source places a requirement later, keep it in the caller's disposition or, if none can hold it, the existing recovery result. Continue only work authorized now; carrying it forward never completes or bypasses it. If the source does not make the activation point clear, report it as unresolved and preserve the stop; do not infer from an incomplete status or label alone. For example, a `Before merge` follow-up remains visible for merge while authorized local implementation continues.
2. Follow caller-named recall first; otherwise search project backlog items and prompt logs (`PLAN-PROMPTS.md`, `EXEC-PROMPTS.md`), lesson corpus, and user-level session memory index for prior dispositions before deciding coverage or duplicating. Report a surface as unavailable when it cannot be consulted; its unavailability alone does not create a new prerequisite. If no existing backlog item or prompt-log entry covers the blocker, create one durable backlog item recording the observed refusal, why the workflow stopped before checking for a workaround, the evidence-backed cause, and the smallest proposed correction. Invoke `investigate` on an open item when no investigation entry or covering plan exists, including an item that predates this run, when the caller's remaining investigation allowance permits it; read its evidence and alternative dispositions before choosing a workaround. If the caller's allowance is spent, leave the item open for the next eligible invocation and continue only with recovery supported by available evidence and existing authority.
3. Carry an explicit workaround direction into the next authorized action: search repository/tool docs, classify the refusal, and re-derive target and authority (method refusal vs missing authority or unsafe target); do not ask the user to repeat it. For slow, failing, missing, or ineffective gates, consult [Dev Quality Gates](https://github.com/mokevnin/dev-quality-gates) and its `docs/adopt.md`; verify current official docs, limits, enforcement and repo fit. Prefer the smallest owner-layer workaround with low blocking risk. For `record_correction_authorization` refusing because the writer identity equals the row's `author_session_id`, classify the corrections-route same-identity refusal. If the blocked session holds the recorded operator direction, its authorized spawn recipe is to spawn a distinct live interactive session with this fixed payload: read the correction row at `<row-path>`, obtain the spawned session's identity from runtime context, and capture the explicit helper result with `python3 scripts/session_channel.py --session-id "$WRITER_SESSION_ID"`; run `record_correction_authorization(repo, row, operator_direction, artifact_path)` from `scripts/corrections_record.py` in a process whose environment passes that captured identity to the helper subprocess and gives it precedence over inherited session identities, where `row` is the parsed row at `<row-path>`, `operator_direction` is the direction of record relayed verbatim, and `artifact_path` is the recorded artifact path; return the `RESULT` and `DETAIL` lines verbatim. The writer contract and guards, state schema, and scheduled-workflow exclusion remain as documented in `scripts/corrections_record.py` and `agents/skills/maintenance/state-file.md`. Never edit or bypass a gate or validator to pass.
4. Apply an authorized workaround, rerun the refused gate, and continue only within the caller's retry and landing rules. For the corrections-route same-identity refusal, after the spawned writer reports its `RESULT` and `DETAIL`, continue only on `RESULT: OK`; the blocked session re-runs its refused verification before dependent work. Relay the direction verbatim from the direction of record; the receipt-directive equality guard is the binding witness. An alternate method must be authorized, then pass the original gate and terminal checks. Record one recovery disposition; keep the origin and investigation prompt open until the root cause is resolved or its owner-required completion witness exists, and close them only through the owning procedure. Never manufacture landing history: an empty-tree marker commit is not a receipt; landing requires content carried by the sanctioned helper and a passing branch-accounting gate. Without that proof, report the failed gate and retain resources unless the user explicitly directs separate local cleanup as described below. Before stopping on an inventory refusal, follow done's Operator branch closeout: transfer unverified items and rerun the gate. Never invent witnesses, edit owner logs, or leave ready work for a completed origin.

   **Retry and disposition check.** Before any retry or terminal disposition, including the initial refusal, freshly check the completion record, current owner, and resource state. Skip only verified peer completion; preserve active or ambiguous ownership. A single refusal records that invocation only and does not prove the caller-owned budget is exhausted. On an explicit renewed request, if the owning workflow permits an attempt, retry the same gate at most once; a stricter budget, including zero, wins. Never auto-repeat, borrow another phase's budget, or bypass the gate.
   A current, explicit user direction covering a named action takes precedence over conflicting lower-level local workflow prose only within the user's authority; higher-priority requirements and action-specific safety still bind. Branch removal after a refused landing is separate from landing: require a named ref, the owning run at authorized closeout with all writers quiesced, a verified archive preserving its full history, no linked worktree, and a fresh exact ref read immediately before expected-OID deletion. Otherwise retain the ref and name the missing evidence; the landing gate remains failed and no landing is claimed.
5. If no safe action is possible without user input, external-owner action or new authorization, preserve the run and report blocker, sources checked, workaround considered, next owner/action, and evidence needed. Ask the user when a decision is required before the next action and the caller authorizes the ask, or when the refusal exposes missing authority, ambiguous ownership, or an unsafe target; route external-owner actions only through the caller's existing authority. Do not treat elapsed time or the user's silence as authorization. An authoring-run continuation is ambiguous ownership when the plans skill's Phase 0 authoring-run continuation ownership rule finds no pass condition: verify that rule before any step 3-4 workaround, and preserve the run's work while the owner-recognized transfer or operator decision is pending.

After `investigate` commits its log entry, mutate the prompt's origin only under the caller's existing owner-recognized freeze or live claim. Do not create machinery merely to avoid a stop: any proposed gate, validator, lock, ledger, or required ceremony must follow the repository's Machinery admission protocol.

## Resource cleanup disposition

A request to clean up agent-created resources ends with one disposition per in-scope resource (every worktree registration or local branch ref the request names or plausibly owns) before any completion claim. Read only the runtime-neutral worktree list, branch ancestry, and owning run or claim records. Build no parallel inventory. The disposition vocabulary:

- **Verified removal**: the resource is gone, confirmed from the inventory sources.
- **Named retention**: the resource stays; the response names its owner (the run or claim record bound to it) and the exact unmerged work it holds.
- **Continuation through the owning authorized closeout**: the resource's disposition belongs to its owning run's authorized closeout; route it there instead of disposing of it in this cleanup.

Remove a linked worktree only on current user direction naming it, after its owner and every writer are quiesced and every tracked, untracked, and ignored item has a verified destination. Otherwise retain it and name the missing direction or evidence. Report worktree removal separately; it never removes its branch ref or turns a refused landing gate into a pass.

A completion claim must disposition every in-scope resource; a partial result is not complete. An unresolved resource ends the request under Recovery step 5.

## Repeated review cycles

When a plans, execute-plan, or other review loop has gone through repeated correction rounds, inspect the chronological review artifacts, sidecars, fix history, and current digest. Use `review-reconciliation` when its recurrence, contradiction, or churn trigger applies. Decide from the evidence whether findings are converging, fixes are producing new defects, the witness is inadequate, or the review surface is misdesigned.

Recommend and carry out the smallest coherent correction that the active workflow already authorizes. If results are converging, continue under that workflow's review budget and exit criteria. If fixes regenerate findings or blocker counts stop improving, simplify or refactor the responsible mechanism before another review when the caller's run-wide attempt state is unused and its bounded allowance permits it; a consumed or unknown state reuses its recorded evidence and pauses without another assessment. Do not reset its allowance or relax its review gate. When reporting to the operator, preserve the evidence and ask only the specific question the stop turns on.

## Integration Points

- `using-skills` routes any skill-workflow stop here for the before-judgment recovery check; user-only decisions stay with the user.
- `plans` calls this skill once before judging a non-converging plan-review loop or an authoring stop unrecoverable; its review budgets and cap-closure rules remain authoritative.
- `done` invokes this handoff once before judging a closeout failure unrecoverable; its fail-closed runner refusals, staging decisions, and user-only stops stay authoritative.
- `done`'s Operator branch closeout report answers a multi-resource cleanup request under `## Resource cleanup disposition`; its sanctioned branch-closeout sequence stays authoritative for each removal.
- `execute-plan` ad-hoc-worktree closeout reports run resource dispositions under `## Resource cleanup disposition` for multi-resource requests; removal gates and Terminal closeout ownership stay authoritative.
- `plans`' landing handoff reports the authoring worktree's and branch's dispositions under `## Resource cleanup disposition` when the handoff answers a multi-resource cleanup request; its landing order stays authoritative and its claim policy stays procedure-owned.
- `plans`' Phase 0 Authoring-run continuation ownership entry boundary owns the ambiguity verdict for authoring-run continuations; Recovery step 5 verifies that rule and preserves the run pending the owner-recognized transfer or operator decision (this skill never restates the pass conditions).
- `execute-plan` uses self-heal before Phase 3 review stops on recurrence, regeneration or escalation/re-entry limits; use it before stopping on blocked authorized closeout. Review-reconciliation, fix-risk and closeout gates govern.
- `review-plan` calls this skill at its active churn or recurrence trigger, before any pause; its one-attempt self-heal state and authorization contract remain authoritative.
- `review-loop` reaches this skill only through `review-plan`'s churn sequence, never a direct call before its standalone fix-risk stop; its review-round, escalation, budget, and authorization limits remain authoritative.
- `maintenance` invokes this skill once per non-user-only blocker before a stop or ask, per `maintenance/decisions.md`; maintenance retains its lane and authorization rules, and owns prompt-log retirement when its existing completion witness is present.
- `unit-test-runner`'s Maven adapter routes test-run blocker recovery here when the private-image or test-service auth handoff does not resolve the failure; the runner's scope classification and reporting duties stay authoritative.
- `investigate` supplies backlog archaeology and alternatives for an unresolved blocker. After its log-entry commit, self-heal continues origin work under an existing owner claim; otherwise it leaves the ready prompt for maintenance and continues only independent recovery. It consumes investigation evidence and never dispatches the prompt.
